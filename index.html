About this text, the English, and sources

About this text. The Krapar (Classical Armenian) text of the verified sections is the Sharagnots of the Armenian Church itself, not a translation, in the corrected-spelling form of the Krapar corpus with the English translations of the Krapar Sharagnots (the hymnal and concordance on which this page is built). Hymns not yet in that corpus appear in the Scan supplement category: their Armenian is the Unicode-anchored reading of the 1888 Etchmiadzin Sharagnots, and every hymn has a button that opens the page of the scan, which is the source of truth.

English. Every hymn stanza shows one English translation, and both kinds are machine-generated and not reviewed by a person: Krapar sections show the English published with the Krapar Sharagnots (AI translation (Krapar Sharagnots)); scan-supplement hymns show AI translation (Claude). Canonical English scripture is the ESV, loaded live from the ACHR website (achrct.org) under the church's Crossway licence; Apocrypha are quoted from the RSVCE with attribution. Readings are the Jashots lectionary readings.

About this site. "Ganon" and other transliterations follow the parish's Maroon Book convention (via TR). Root/declension search is powered by uniparser-classical-armenian. Hymn-type genres follow Fr. Daniel Findikyan's "Armenian Hymnography" (St. Nersess Armenian Seminary, LS323). Questions or corrections: frharoutiun@gmail.com.
